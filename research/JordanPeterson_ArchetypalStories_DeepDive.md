## 1. The framework — what Peterson actually claims

**The founding move (Peterson's-own).** The world can be validly construed two ways: as a *place
of things* (the domain of science — "what is") and as a *forum for action* (the domain of
narrative and myth — "what is and what should be," bridged by "how should we act?"). Myth is not
a primitive description of objects; it is a **map of meaning** — it encodes the motivational
significance of things *for action*. This is the load-bearing premise, and it is Peterson's own
synthetic interpretation, not an established result.

**The two territories (Peterson's-own, built on Jung/Eliade/Neumann).** Lived experience is
organized by **order** (the known, explored territory) and **chaos** (the unknown, unexplored
territory), with a third category, the **anomalous** — what breaks the existing map. The unknown
is the source of both terror and all new value.

**The archetypal actors (Peterson's-own, on scholarly-secondary inheritances).**
- **The Great Father** — order; "culture, protective and tyrannical; cumulative ancestral wisdom"
  that shelters but ossifies into tyranny.
- **The Great Mother** — chaos/nature; "creative and destructive, source and final resting place
  of all determinate things."
- **The Divine Son / the Hero** — the exploratory process that mediates the two: "ride the
  border," renewing order without collapsing into chaos.
- **The Hostile Brothers** (final chapter of *Maps of Meaning*) — the hero/Christ-figure ("the
  capacity to admit the unknown") versus the adversary/Satan ("the eternal rejection of the
  redeeming unknown"); Cain and Abel as the two orientations of one psyche.

**The dragon of chaos (Peterson's-own).** The unknown personified is the dragon/serpent; the hero
*voluntarily* confronts it and extracts value — "Why do dragons hoard gold? Because the thing you
most need is always to be found where you least want to look."

**The scientific grounding bid (Peterson's-own, citing established work).** Meaning is built
bottom-up through action before it is ever stated: "Behavior is imitated, then abstracted into
play, formalized into drama and story, crystallized into myth and codified into religion." He
roots this in **Piaget's** action-schemas (procedural before declarative knowledge) and gestures
at neuropsychology (hippocampal novelty processing). The *integration* — myth as the codification
of behavioral/neural regularities — is his interpretation, not a peer-reviewed finding.

**Into the later, biblical work.**
- *12 Rules / Beyond Order* are **applied mythology**: "Stand up straight" routes order/chaos
  through the (contested) dominance-hierarchy biology; "Pursue what is meaningful, not what is
  expedient" is read straight off Cain and Abel.
- The **Bible** is framed as the foundational archetypal text of the West — "the ancient,
  foundational stories" whose patterns "stabilize, inspire, and unite us."
- The **Logos** (Genesis lectures): "God said" is the Word that "calls being into existence,"
  shaping "habitable order out of precosmogonic chaos." Truthful speech is world-creating; the
  Logos is the antidote to chaos.
- *We Who Wrestle With God* (2024): the title-archetype, Jacob wrestling the angel, is the thesis —
  maturity requires *wrestling* with the highest value and being wounded and renamed by it.
- The bridge to life: belief is **enacted**, not declared — "I act as if God exists… and I'm
  terrified that He might." Religious truth is "true" insofar as it is the deepest pattern one must
  live out. This pragmatic move is also exactly what critics name as the unresolved center.

**Source-class summary:** the architecture is Peterson's **own theoretical synthesis** atop
scholarly-secondary sources (Jung, Neumann, Eliade, Frye, Solzhenitsyn) and established science
(Piaget, neuropsychology). **None of the archetypal claims are themselves peer-reviewed findings.**

## 2. The lineage — debts and departures

- **Jung** — the deepest debt (collective unconscious, archetypes, individuation, shadow, Self).
  *Charged departure:* Peterson hardens Jung's *descriptive, ambivalent* archetypes into *causal,
  moralized, gendered* forces ("colour and characterise rather than cause and define" is Jung; the
  moralized reading "is Petersonian, not Jungian"). *(critical: Waller — provisional, 403.)*
- **Erich Neumann** — arguably the truer structural source: *The Origins and History of
  Consciousness* maps consciousness through Great Mother → Hero → Dragon-slaying. Peterson's
  order/chaos/hero "deep grammar" tracks Neumann more than Jung directly. *(scholarly-secondary.)*
- **Eliade** — sacred/profane, cosmos vs. chaos, moderns unconsciously inhabiting sacred
  structure. *(scholarly-secondary.)*
- **Campbell** — the monomyth, reframed: Peterson's distinctive **exploratory hero** converts the
  unknown into the "expected, desired, predictable" — an information-processing reading rather than
  Campbell's departure–initiation–return. Inherits Campbell's selection-bias vulnerability.
- **Frye** — *The Great Code* (the Bible as the unified mythological substrate of Western
  literature) licenses reading scripture as the master code. *(scholarly-secondary.)*
- **Piaget** — least-credited, load-bearing: meaning built from action-schemas "from the bottom
  up," equilibration/disequilibrium as the engine by which anomaly forces re-mapping. This is what
  lets Peterson claim scientific grounding. *(Peterson's-own paper.)*
- **Dostoevsky & Nietzsche** — the diagnosticians of nihilism. *Charged misreading:* Peterson
  aligns Nietzsche closer to the nihilism he diagnosed; critics call this "founded on a
  misreading." *(critical.)*
- **Evolutionary psych / neuroscience** — the **lobster/dominance-hierarchy** argument (weakest)
  and **Panksepp's** affective-neuroscience systems (sturdiest scientific warrant).

**Pattern:** Peterson is **most faithful where he is least famous** (Neumann, Frye, Piaget,
Panksepp) and **most exposed where he is most quoted** (Jung-hardening, the gendered chaos/order
mapping, the lobster homology, the Nietzsche reading). A synthesizer assimilating disparate
sources into one order/chaos/hero grammar — powerful as architecture, vulnerable wherever a single
source is pressed past what it claims.

## 3. The steel-man — the strongest defensible version, and its backing

Drop Jung entirely, and a robust thesis survives on three converging **peer-reviewed** lines:

1. **Narrative as evolved social technology.** Storytelling is a human universal, appears
   spontaneously in childhood, and is ancient. Smith et al. (2017) found hunter-gatherer stories
   "coordinate group behaviour and facilitate cooperation by providing… social information about
   the norms, rules and expectations in a given society" — direct support for "stories transmit
   hard-won behavioral patterns." da Silva & Tehrani (2016) traced some folktales to the Bronze
   Age (deep antiquity + faithful transmission). *(peer-reviewed.)*
2. **Cross-cultural recurrence of story patterns.** Gottschall's content analysis across 48
   culture areas; Hogan's corpus work on a limited set of emotion-grounded prototypical
   structures; the Aarne–Thompson–Uther type-index. Recurrence is real; *cause and tightness* are
   the live questions. *(peer-reviewed / scholarly-secondary.)*
3. **Meaning, narrative identity, and well-being** — his empirically strongest flank, and not his
   own work. McAdams's narrative-identity research links redemptive, agentic life-stories to
   well-being; terror-management theory (1,500+ studies) and the Meaning-Maintenance Model show
   meaning-frameworks buffer existential threat; Wong (2019, peer-reviewed) credits Peterson's
   emphasis on responsibility, meaning, and suffering as a genuine contribution. *(peer-reviewed.)*

**So the steel-man holds:** stories are an adaptive cognitive technology, certain structures recur
across cultures, and meaning-frameworks measurably aid well-being — none of which needs Jung.

## 4. The critique — symmetric (what survives, what overreaches)

**Critiques that survive scrutiny (the narrow, methodological ones):**
- **Selection bias / monomyth-flattening.** Folklorists reject Campbell's universal monomyth for
  source-selection bias — Toelken: he "could construct a monomyth… only by citing those stories
  that fit his preconceived mold"; Dundes: "no single idea… has done more harm to serious folklore
  study than the notion of archetype." Plus the emic/etic problem (the same motif means different
  things in different cultures). Transfers cleanly to Peterson. *(scholarly-secondary / critical.)*
- **The lobster *inference* (not the metaphor).** Gonçalves (UCL): vast neural distance; humans
  don't use octopamine as a major CNS modulator; the serotonin–aggression link in some respects
  runs *opposite* in humans. *Concession:* serotonin does modulate dominance posture in
  lobsters/crayfish, and hierarchies are genuinely ancient. The fault is the **disanalogy** — the
  inference that lobster neurobiology makes human hierarchy natural/inevitable — not the existence
  of ancient hierarchy. *(critical-popular, expert-authored.)*
- **Anachronistic biblical reading.** Independently flagged by a **secular** reviewer (Meyer,
  *Freethinker*: readings "mainly metaphorical, though the originators may well have intended their
  stories more literally"), a **Reformed** scholar (Piotrowski: Peterson treats "abstracted
  Jungian archetypes as the authorial intent of the text"), and partly a **leftist** one. The
  convergence across hostile camps is what survives: Jungian-as-authorial-intent + monomyth-
  flattening of distinct texts. *(secular-critical / confessional-scholarly.)*
- **Minimal scholarly uptake of *Maps of Meaning* specifically** — ~270 Google Scholar citations
  (vs. thousands for same-year peers); largely ignored by the fields it claimed to contribute to.
  *(encyclopedic / critical.)*
- **Jungian metaphysics presented as settled, and the pragmatic theory of "truth."** The
  collective unconscious is unproven and arguably unfalsifiable; defining truth Darwinically
  ("what conduces to survival is true") is contestable philosophy, not science — Harris's standard
  objection (survival can't ground truth). *(critical.)*

**Critiques that themselves overreach (Rule 3 applied to the critics):**
- **"No innate narrative predispositions exist" — false.** A contested-but-**legitimate
  peer-reviewed** program reframes archetypes as evolved predispositions: Stevens (*Archetype: A
  Natural History of the Self*), Goodwyn ("Recurrent motifs as resonant attractor states," *J.
  Analytical Psychology* 2013), Brown's *Human Universals*, da Silva & Tehrani. The real dispute is
  about **mechanism** (innate vs. developmental), not whether recurrent predispositions exist.
- **"Peterson isn't a real scholar" — false.** ~26,000 citations, mostly co-authored empirical
  personality work (DeYoung, Quilty & Peterson 2007, *JPSP*). The accurate claim is narrower: *his
  myth-theorizing* had little uptake, not that he is not a scientist.
- **The confessional "he got the doctrine wrong" complaints** — largely a **category error**:
  faulting Peterson for not doing redemptive theology he explicitly disclaims.
- **The "Documentary Hypothesis presented as settled fact" charge** — the critic stands on
  contested ground too; the DH has been heavily revised.

**Recurring pattern:** the critics are usually **right about Peterson's inferences and
overgeneralizations** and **wrong when they deny the kernel** he builds on.

## 5. Calibration — the honest split

- **Well-supported (peer-reviewed):** storytelling as cooperation/norm-transmission; deep
  antiquity of tale-structures; cross-cultural recurrence (with debated cause); the
  meaning/narrative-identity → well-being link.
- **Peterson's interpretation (defensible reading, not fact):** the Jungian overlay; myths as
  pragmatically "true"; the universality of the monomyth; his specific archetypal cast as *the*
  structure.
- **Genuinely contested:** archetypes as biological/inherited (a live mechanism dispute, not a
  closed question); the historical/theological claims about scripture.

**Bottom line.** Strip the Jungian biology and the survival-theory of truth, and a strong thesis
remains: enduring stories are an adaptive cognitive technology, certain structures recur across
cultures, and meaning-frameworks aid well-being. The weakness is not the project but the **unearned
certainty** Peterson attaches to its most metaphysical parts — and, symmetrically, his critics are
right about the overreach and wrong when they deny the kernel.

# ELI

A novel. **Older work** — predates the recent ~8-week sprint, and more worked
than the sprint material (ELI's prose is the corpus's quality benchmark).

- Parts One ("The Swamp") and Two ("The Formation") are drafted — the formation
  of a witness (the swamp method, "the space between terror and action," the
  women, Rebeca as "the center that cannot be entered").
- **Sara: The Morning Protocol** is an embedded chapter (marked "Final Draft —
  Complete"): the atrocity the witness witnesses — coercive-control/trafficking,
  graphic. Craft note on file: the explicit register currently overruns the
  witnessing consciousness; the hinge of the book is whether Sara reads as a soul
  the reader grieves, not a body consumed.
- **Narrative present not yet drafted.** The inherited-Sara choice ("no right
  answer") and the redemptive ending (Sara and their daughter arrive to take the
  old man to a birthday dinner — the María Cristina garden, the Holy Year) exist
  in plan. The novel's potential is real; its realization waits here.
